# Korean cartridge entry and header logo

The official Korean candidate is analyzed after the Japanese origin reference. Its fixed cartridge entry targets `$05ca`, while the Japanese revision-zero entry targets `$05c5`; this regional difference is preserved in separate RGBDS source and analysis. The Korean header logo decodes to a visually verified 384×64 PNG and hashes identically to the Japanese logo. The public report omits raw ROM byte extracts and retains source-slice hashes and provenance. Candidate status is unchanged.
