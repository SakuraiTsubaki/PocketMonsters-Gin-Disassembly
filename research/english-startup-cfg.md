# English startup control flow

The official English Silver candidate is analyzed after the Japanese origin and Korean build. Its entry reaches `$05c6`. The CGB and non-CGB paths converge at `$05cf`, followed by register initialization and the WRAM-clear loop at `$05fc`.

The depth-two report and generated RGBDS source come from the English ROM itself. The report keeps addresses, instruction semantics, graph edges, and SHA-256 evidence while omitting verbatim ROM byte strings. Candidate status is unchanged.
