# Pokemon Silver Pokémon footprints

The Japanese release `gin-jp-rev0` is the origin reference. The complete 8,192-byte footprint table is byte-identical in all 8 verified releases (Japanese, Korean, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian), including Korean where officially available. It contains 256 four-tile slots: 251 species and five unused slots.

The ROM range is rendered as `graphics/pokemon/footprints-atlas.png`; `footprints-atlas.json` maps every numbered slot to logical and scaled PNG rectangles. Reports retain release identities, offsets, hashes, and provenance without publishing raw ROM bytes. Naming and storage order follow `pret/pokegold` at `ef0201d8daf47e8b3ea1518eacf890f37d4cd5e8`, `gfx/footprints.asm`.
