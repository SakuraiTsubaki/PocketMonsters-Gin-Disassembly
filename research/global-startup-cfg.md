# European startup control flow

After the Japanese origin, Korean, and English builds, the official German, French, Italian, and Spanish Silver ROMs were independently decoded from their verified release hashes. All four preserve the English startup boundaries at `$05c6`, `$05ca`, `$05cd`, `$05cf`, and `$05fc` and have matching block SHA-256 values.

This is a verified shared implementation, not substituted evidence: every language retains its own publication-safe analysis report, generated RGBDS source, ROM identity, range hashes, and manifest. Candidate status is unchanged and no verbatim ROM byte strings are published.
