# Gin delay routines

`gin-jp-rev0` is the Japanese origin reference. All 8 cataloged releases (Japanese, Korean, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian) contain one structurally verified ROM0 `DelayFrame` / `DelayFrames` pair. The reconstruction records the release-specific VBlank flag address and exact routine hash without publishing ROM bytes. Korean builds remain ahead of English in the release order where an official Korean version exists. Release candidate/verified status is preserved and is not promoted by this match.
