# Pokemon Silver Unown font

The Japanese release `gin-jp-rev0` is the origin reference. Its 27 uncompressed 2bpp tiles are byte-identical in all 8 verified releases (Japanese, Korean, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian), including the official Korean release where available. The two bitplanes are identical, producing a monochrome glyph set.

`graphics/font/unown-font.png` is regenerated directly from the local ROM range. Per-release reports preserve ROM identity, offsets, source and PNG hashes, while no raw ROM bytes are published. Naming is corroborated by `pret/pokegold` at `ef0201d8daf47e8b3ea1518eacf890f37d4cd5e8`, `gfx/font.asm`.
