# Source Conflicts — Pocket Monsters Gin / Pokémon Silver

Started: 2026-09-14

This file records factual conflicts found during the public-source census. Conflicts are not silently resolved by majority repetition; copied/derivative sources are not treated as independent proof.

## KR-RELEASE-DATE-001 — Korean retail release date

### Claim A: 2002-04-24

Public sources found in this pass that give or repeat **24 April 2002** include:

- GameMeca game database: lists release as `2002.04.24` and provider/distributor as Daewon C.I.
- Inven retrospective on Pokémon history: states the Korean Gold/Silver localization was officially released on 2002-04-24 through Daewon C.I.
- Korean Wikipedia and several derivative/community references repeat the same date.

### Claim B: May 2002

A 2003-11-06 Edaily article reporting Daewon C.I.'s announcement that Korean Gold/Silver had passed 40,000 domestic sales quotes a Daewon team manager and describes the title as having been released domestically "last May" (`지난해 5월`).

### Assessment

`UNRESOLVED`.

The Edaily wording may be an approximate month reference rather than an exact launch-date statement, but it is closer in time and tied to a company sales announcement. Conversely, 2002-04-24 is repeated by multiple later game-history/database sources. Repetition alone does not establish independent provenance.

### Required primary-source search

- Contemporary Daewon C.I./Daewon Game announcement or product sheet.
- April–May 2002 Korean game-magazine advertisements and release calendars.
- Contemporary retailer catalogue/order sheet.
- Korean box/manual/campaign material containing publication/release metadata.
- Archived Korean web pages from Daewon or the distributor, if recoverable.
- Contemporary 2002 news/review publication date and wording.

### Current repository rule

Until a stronger contemporary source is found, do not label `2002-04-24` as primary-source verified. It may be shown as the commonly reported exact date with this conflict attached.
